TL;DR for Leadership

Customer complaints are valid. While 99–100% of calls are being processed by the AI, the percentage receiving a full scorecard/grade ("Analyzed") has declined for several clients.

Three patterns: (1) complete pipeline failures for specific clients, (2) gradual rate decline suggesting a classification shift, (3) one client with a partial recovery after a processing outage.

Key Distinction: "Evaluated" vs "Analyzed"

Evaluated = call processed by the AI system (99.4–100% every month — healthy).

Analyzed = call receives a full AI scorecard/grade (~8% of calls — this is what's declining).

Customers perceive "graded calls" as the Analyzed number — that's what's dropping.

Root Cause Hypotheses

1. Call Classification Threshold Change

High Confidence

The system "Evaluates" (processes) 99–100% of calls but only "Analyzes" (fully grades) ~8%. If the criteria for what triggers a full analysis tightened — perhaps a model update or threshold change around what qualifies as a "scoreable lead call" — you'd see exactly this pattern: total calls up, evaluated at 100%, but analyzed rate declining.

Evidence: Benchmark's clean cutoff at exactly March (from 9.7% to 0%) suggests a deploy, not a gradual drift.

2. Call Type Mix Shift

Medium Confidence

If clients are receiving more non-lead calls (maintenance, existing resident, vendor), those would be "Evaluated" but not qualify for "Analysis." Rising total volume from non-lead calls dilutes the analyzed rate.

Evidence: Clients with gradual declines (Burkentine, Klingbeil) are consistent with a shifting call mix rather than a hard break.

3. Client-Specific Pipeline Failures

High Confidence

Benchmark going to exactly 0% for 4 straight months is not a gradual trend — it's a break. Likely a config issue, disabled integration, or classification rule that's misrouting their calls away from the analysis engine entirely.

Evidence: Only 2–3 clients are at exactly 0%. The rest have non-zero (even if low) rates, ruling out a system-wide failure.

Recommended Investigation Steps

  1. Benchmark pipeline audit — Check configuration for why they've had 0 analyzed calls since March. This should be a quick fix.
  2. March deploy audit — Identify any model updates, threshold changes, or classification rule changes deployed between Feb 28 and Mar 5, 2026.
  3. Call type breakdown for decliners — Pull call classification (lead vs non-lead) distribution for Burkentine, Klingbeil, and Advanced Management to see if their call mix shifted.
  4. Horizon root cause — Document what caused Feb–Apr evaluation failures and why analysis hasn't fully recovered despite evaluation being fixed.
  5. Analyze "Analyzed" criteria — Confirm what determines whether an Evaluated call gets promoted to Analyzed. Is this threshold documented? Has it changed?
  6. May spike investigation — May had the highest analyzed rate (9.6%) then June crashed to 7.9%. What was different about May?
